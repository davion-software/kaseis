# Onboard a pilot obra

Target about 48 hours of assisted setup for a suitable design partner. This checklist is planned; the app and import tools do not exist yet. Use [pilot scope](../PILOT_SCOPE.md) and resolve the blocking [open questions](../OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) before live inventory writes.

1. Confirm the partner, one obra, 20–50 critical materials, and 3–5 active users. Review current Excel or notebook records.
2. Identify who registers, approves, and administers. Record approval authority before accepting a baseline or adjustment.
3. Prepare the organization and obra. Agree the pilot's location policy before setup.
4. Review catalog names, aliases, base units, packaging conversions, and minimum stock levels with the field team. Assisted catalog loading does not establish a self-service Excel import feature.
5. Link authorized users and WhatsApp numbers. Verify that offboarded users can lose access immediately.
6. Record and approve a physical starting count. Keep the approver and evidence traceable.
7. Train each role for 20–30 minutes on CONFIRMAR, CORREGIR, CANCELAR, stock queries, and reporting a difference.
8. Demonstrate one ingreso and salida, including a duplicate confirmation that changes no additional stock.
9. Agree daily review, weekly physical reconciliation, and who handles pending items. Define how pilot success metrics will be collected.

The obra is ready only when the approved baseline, authorized WhatsApp flow, stock explanation, and review ownership are demonstrated. Record failures and owners rather than declaring the checklist complete.
