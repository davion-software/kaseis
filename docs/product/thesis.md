# Product thesis

Kaseis Obras is a vertical SaaS for materials and supply control in Latin American construction. The first wedge is **Inventory Truth**: turn informal field records into a reliable, reconciliable, auditable inventory.

## Promise

Field teams register movements on WhatsApp (via Kapso). Residents, admins, and owners get visibility on the web dashboard to request, receive, consume, and control materials.

## What we sell

Not a chatbot and not a digital kardex alone. We sell **operational trust**: verified balances, visible uncertainty, and evidence. Automations without that trust have little value.

## Entry loop (pilot)

1. Configure one obra and a controlled catalog of critical materials
2. Establish inventory baseline via approved physical count
3. Capture ingresos/salidas/devoluciones/ajustes with confirmation
4. Query stock and low materials from WhatsApp
5. Run guided counts and weekly reconciliation
6. Act on alerts and differences from the dashboard

## Principles

- **Trust before automation** — a doubtful figure is worse than an incomplete one
- **Field first** — fit obra language and pressure; confirm before changing stock
- **Ledger, not silent edits** — reverse or compensate; never rewrite confirmed movements
- **Exceptions visible** — differences and pending items beat decorative charts
- **Hybrid interpretation** — deterministic rules first; LLM only as fallback; never sole authority for stock

## Expansion (after pilot PMF)

Request-to-Receive → Cost Control → Procure-to-Pay → Materials Intelligence. Workforce / CAPECO are parallel products, not the core materials roadmap.
